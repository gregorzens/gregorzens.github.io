## Teaching and Supervision

My teaching spans undergraduate courses in statistics, econometrics, data science, economic development, and macroeconomic theory, as well as graduate-level lectures on macroeconometric methods, with a particular emphasis on Bayesian computation.

I supervise Bachelor’s and Master’s theses and contribute to the training of PhD students through doctoral summer schools. Recent projects have included using text mining to study inflation expectations on Reddit, nowcasting GDP growth with Google Trends, and modelling European migration flows using social-media network data.
